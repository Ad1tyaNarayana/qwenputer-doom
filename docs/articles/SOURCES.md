# Evidence ledger for Doom on Qwenputer

This ledger covers the article and its figures. Paths are relative to the repository root. JSON selectors use dot notation, brackets for arrays, and `*` for every member of a named group. Repeated and rounded occurrences share a row. Numbers inside identifiers such as RV32IM, Qwen3.5, `F0`, fp32, RGB565, filenames, and section labels are names, not additional performance measurements.

“Measurement” below includes recorded execution counts, exact comparisons, circuit inventories, and linked-object sizes. “Derived” means arithmetic over recorded evidence. “Estimate” means applying a measured rate to another workload or assuming an unimplemented architecture. A target is a design requirement, never a measurement.

## Source reconciliations and open work

- The original ROM README first records failure and no timing. Its diagnosis and re-verification sections supersede those statuses for explicitly identified fixed source revisions. The article keeps the failed attempt, 1.889× first fixed timing, 1.902× revised timing, and later three-way timing distinct.
- The later three-way ROM-only median is about 251.7 µs/token, versus about 252.1 in revised-ROM's own benchmark and about 253.7 for the earlier barrier fix. These are separate measurements. The article does not mix them to construct a matched speedup.
- Historical original-profile and later normal-path medians differ slightly, and instrumented stream spans exceed wall µs/token. The first chart deliberately compares separate profiler captures and says so. It is not the decomposition of the second chart.
- `dmc-cuda-profile/README.md` originally says `FreeRun.load(GO)` resets histories. The ROM diagnosis and `docs/verification.md` supersede that claim because indexed reset zeros a copy. The historical first-frame prefix also retained warm-up histories. Recorded zero token mismatches and frame/RAM comparisons remain valid; fresh-zero-history startup is unestablished for that run.
- The reported 1% “memory utilization” is not a measured peak-bandwidth fraction. The article uses the observed metric and declines the requested shorthand “memory bandwidth ~1%”. No bandwidth number is invented.
- The 52,000× gap is a derived original-executor continuing-workload projection. After fusion, the same calculation is about 24,650×. The handoff's 73,070× uses the historical first frame and cannot be labelled steady state.
- A phase-count JSON exists locally at `.cache/dmc-continuing/final-run/report.json`, but it is private/ignored and is not a dependency of this article's generator. Published `CONTINUING.md` provides the phase numbers. The suggested phase chart is replaced by instruction mix from published `counts-walk.json`. No private state, trace, pixels, or game-bearing artifacts are copied.
- The design warns that some older walk reports put cumulative counts in `frames[].tokens` and `frames[].instructions`. The article uses the corrected mean already exported in `cost-model.json` and the published whole-run phase table. It does not average unverified frame fields. Public `counts-walk.json` and the local corrected report have different ELF hashes; global counts agree, but their identities must not be silently treated as interchangeable.
- Complete fused first-frame generation, uninterrupted wall time, and clean-reset provenance are now measured for both new runs. No rerun or verification markers remain. Continuing neural frames remain unmeasured and appear only as PRIVATE image comments, not pending numerical table entries.
